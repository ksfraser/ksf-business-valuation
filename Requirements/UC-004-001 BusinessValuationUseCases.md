# UC-004-001 BusinessValuationUseCases: Business Valuation Use Cases

**Related:** BR-004 Business Valuation, FR-004-001..005

## Actors
- **Advisor** — FA staff user managing a client's plan.
- **Client** — FA customer (debtor_no) whose plan is being built.

## Use cases
- **UC-004-001 Business Valuation Engine** — Value a business for planning or sale.
- **UC-004-002 Buy Sell Agreement Analyzer** — Analyze funding needs of a buy-sell agreement.
- **UC-004-003 Succession Planning Engine** — Plan leadership / family-business succession.
- **UC-004-004 Overhead Expense Calculator** — Compute business overhead for valuation.
- **UC-004-005 Cost Benefit Calculator** — Compare costs vs benefits of planning options.
